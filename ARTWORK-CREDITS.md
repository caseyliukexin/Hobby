## Version notes

- The Sower is KM 106.399 / F422, June 1888, with a large sun and blue-violet field; not the November tree-trunk version.
- The seascape is VGM s0117V1962 / F415, with expressive foreground waves; not Fishing Boats on the Beach. The museum calls it Seascape near Les Saintes-Maries-de-la-Mer.
- Self-Portrait is Musée d’Orsay F627, 1889, with blue-green swirling background.
- Road with Cypress and Star is F683, titled Country Road in Provence by Night by Kröller-Müller Museum.
- Olive Trees with Yellow Sky and Sun is F710, Minneapolis 51.7; museum title is Olive Trees.
- Tree Roots is F816, VGM s0195V1962, July 1890. No death/tragedy interpretation was added.
- Courting Couples uses the museum faithful reproduction, not the digitally reconstructed My Dream variant.
- Original short visual descriptions are factual presentation copy, not curator notes.
